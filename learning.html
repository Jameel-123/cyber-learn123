Learning Centre

This page provides beginner-friendly cybersecurity learning material. Read through each section to build your understanding before attempting the quizzes.

1. Cybersecurity Basics

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting systems, networks, and data from unauthorised access, damage, or misuse.

2. Ethical Hacking

Ethical hacking means testing systems with permission to find weaknesses before attackers can abuse them.

3. Networking Basics

Networks allow devices to communicate. Important concepts include IP addresses, ports, protocols, DNS, routers, and firewalls.

4. Penetration Testing

Penetration testing follows stages such as planning, reconnaissance, scanning, analysis, reporting, and remediation.

Penetration Testing Lifecycle

1. Planning and Scope

Before testing begins, the tester must understand what systems are allowed to be tested. This is known as the scope.

2. Reconnaissance

Reconnaissance means gathering information about the target environment. This can include domain names, IP addresses, services, and public information.

3. Scanning

Scanning is used to identify active hosts, open ports, and running services. This helps understand what is exposed.

4. Analysis

After scanning, results are reviewed to identify potential weaknesses, misconfigurations, or risky services.

5. Reporting

Findings should be documented clearly, including risk level, evidence, and recommended fixes.

Key Terms

  • Vulnerability: A weakness that could be exploited.
  • Exploit: A method used to take advantage of a weakness.
  • Risk: The chance and impact of something going wrong.
  • Patch: An update used to fix a security issue.
  • Firewall: A control that filters network traffic.
