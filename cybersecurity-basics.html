Page 1 - Introduction to Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity is the process of protecting systems, devices, networks, and data from cyber threats and unauthorised access.

Modern organisations rely heavily on technology, making cybersecurity extremely important.

Security professionals work to:

  • Protect sensitive data
  • Prevent cyber attacks
  • Monitor suspicious activity
  • Respond to incidents
Example: Antivirus + Firewalls + Password Security

Page 2 - The CIA Triad

The CIA Triad is one of the most important concepts in cybersecurity.

Confidentiality

Confidentiality means keeping information private.

Example: Passwords and encryption

Integrity

Integrity ensures information stays accurate and unchanged.

Example: File hashing

Availability

Availability ensures systems remain accessible.

Example: Backups and redundancy

Page 3 - Common Cyber Threats

Malware

Malware is malicious software designed to damage systems.

  • Viruses
  • Ransomware
  • Spyware
  • Trojans

Phishing

Phishing attacks trick users into revealing sensitive information.

Never click suspicious links or unknown attachments.

Social Engineering

Social engineering manipulates people rather than systems.

Page 4 - Password Security

Strong passwords are one of the most important security controls.

Good Password Practices

  • Use long passwords
  • Use upper and lowercase letters
  • Include numbers and symbols
  • Avoid common words
  • Do not reuse passwords
Example Strong Password: Cyb3r!Learn2026#

Multi-Factor Authentication

MFA adds additional security by requiring extra verification.

Page 5 - Ethical Hacking

Ethical hacking means testing systems legally and with permission.

Ethical hackers identify weaknesses before attackers can exploit them.

Ethical hacking should only be performed with proper authorisation.
  • Remain within scope
  • Protect collected information
  • Report findings professionally
